Managing your finances effectively and efficiently - chasing payments while protecting the relationships that matter most to your business.

Get in Touch

At Bypress, our credit control service is all about the human touch. We understand that managing credit and collections is not just about numbers - it's about building relationships and fostering trust. Our team takes the time to get to know your clients, ensuring that communications are respectful and understanding. We don't just chase payments; we engage in meaningful conversations that help maintain strong business relationships while also protecting your cash flow.

Why Outsource Credit Control?
Improved Cash Flow

Without a healthy cash flow your company can't run effectively. At Bypress we aim to ensure your money is collected to terms, keeping your business financially healthy and predictable.

Formalised Process

We follow monthly procedures that proactively remind your customers to pay on time - including letter cycles, proactive calls, query management and customer credit monitoring.

Expert Team

When dealing with your money and cash flow, you need the best people. Our staff have a great track record, deep industry experience, and a professional, customer-focused attitude.

Cost Effective

Employing an in-house credit controller costs €30,000+ a year. With Bypress, it's like having your own credit controller - without the desk space - at a fraction of the cost.

Aged Debt Recovery

We work through and recover your aged debt too - assisting you through the legal process, from formal debt recovery steps right through to legal action, making the process stress-free.

Less Stress

We take the strain of collecting your money, freeing you to focus on customers, grow sales, and run your business. We also advise on credit worthiness and payment terms for new customers.
