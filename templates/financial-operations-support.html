Helping businesses stay financially organised and improve cashflow.

Managing the financial side of a business can quickly become time-consuming and difficult to stay on top of, particularly as workloads increase and businesses grow. Delayed invoicing, inconsistent payment follow-up, outstanding debtor issues, and poor financial visibility can all place unnecessary pressure on cashflow and day-to-day operations.

At Bypress, we provide structured finance and credit support that helps businesses stay organised, improve consistency, and maintain stronger control over their financial processes. From invoicing and accounts administration to credit control and debtor management, we help ensure that important financial tasks are completed accurately, efficiently, and on time.

Our role is to support businesses behind the scenes by improving financial organisation, reducing operational pressure, and helping clients maintain healthier cashflow through proactive follow-up and streamlined processes.

Services Include
Invoicing

Professional preparation and sending of customer invoices accurately and on time, helping businesses improve cashflow, reduce delays in payment, maintain strong customer communication, and ensure all billing information is organised, consistent, and easy to track.

Credit control

Proactive management of outstanding payments through structured follow-up, customer communication, and account monitoring to reduce overdue debt, improve cashflow, strengthen payment performance, and maintain positive customer relationships professionally.

Debtor management

Complete oversight of debtor accounts including monitoring balances, tracking payment trends, resolving queries, and ensuring consistent communication with customers to improve collections, reduce aged debt, and support healthier business cashflow.

Accounts administration

Day-to-day support with financial administration tasks including account updates, document management, reporting assistance, payment tracking, and maintaining organised records to help businesses run efficiently and stay fully up to date.

Payment follow-up

Consistent and professional follow-up on overdue invoices through phone calls, emails, and account reviews to encourage timely payment, reduce debtor days, improve collections performance, and maintain strong customer relationships.

Bank reconciliations

Accurate reconciliation of bank accounts against company records to identify discrepancies, track transactions, ensure financial accuracy, improve reporting reliability, and provide businesses with a clearer understanding of their finances.

Bookkeeping support

Reliable bookkeeping support including transaction processing, record maintenance, account organisation, and financial data management to help businesses stay compliant, organised, and prepared for reporting, VAT, and year-end requirements.

Cashflow visibility

Clear reporting and monitoring of incoming and outgoing funds to help businesses understand their financial position, plan ahead confidently, identify risks early, improve decision-making, and maintain stronger overall cashflow control.
