Common Questions

Is the card terminal really free?

Some provider plans may offer a terminal with no monthly rental. Transaction fees and other usage-related costs may still apply. We will explain the full cost before you apply.

Can I use it without Wi-Fi?

Some terminals include mobile connectivity, which can make them suitable for mobile businesses, markets, events and locations without reliable Wi-Fi.

How quickly can I receive the terminal?

Timelines depend on the provider, approval process and delivery. Once we know your business details, we can give you a clearer estimate.

Can I get more than one terminal?

This depends on your business locations, turnover and provider terms. If you need multiple devices, tell us in the form and we can review suitable options.

Can I cancel if I no longer need it?

Cancellation terms depend on the provider agreement. We will explain the relevant conditions before you proceed.
