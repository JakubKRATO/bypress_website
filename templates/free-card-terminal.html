Questions

Common Questions

Can I really get a free card machine?

Yes. The offer is for a card machine plan with €0/month terminal rental. Transaction rates and other usage-related costs may still apply.

Who is this offer best suited for?

It can be a strong fit for seasonal businesses, lower-card-turnover businesses, market sellers, taxi drivers, food trucks, cafes, charities, barbers, salons, therapists, trades and similar businesses.

How many free card machines can I get?

The offer is usually limited to one free card machine per site. If you have multiple business locations, you may be able to apply for one per location. If you need multiple terminals at the same site, ask for a quote.

Can I cancel?

The card machine agreement is €0/month. If you no longer want it, you can send the card machine back.

What happens if the card machine breaks?

A replacement device can be sent to you.
